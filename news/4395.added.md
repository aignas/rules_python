(pypi) Hubs created with {obj}`pip.parse.pyproject_toml` now only expose the packages listed in the `pyproject.toml`. The new {obj}`pip.configure` tag selects the exposed packages from `pyproject.toml` dependency groups, either in place or as separate venv repositories, and can mark packages as testonly or expose their transitive dependencies from {obj}`pip.parse.uv_lock`.
